Moonpaper

Support

Moonpaper provides live Solana token research and a manual link to the selected token in FOMO.

Live data is unavailable

Pull down to refresh and check your internet connection. Provider rate limits or Solana RPC congestion can temporarily make one field unavailable; Moonpaper shows missing information instead of inventing it.

FOMO opens its login page

Sign in to FOMO, return to Moonpaper, and tap the manual trade button again. Moonpaper cannot access your FOMO account or complete a trade for you.

Report a problem

Open a ticket in the Moonpaper GitHub issue tracker. Include the app version, iOS version, token mint, and what you expected to happen. Never include a password, private key, or seed phrase.
