Moonpaper is a decision-support prototype. Every trade shown here is a simulation: no real funds are used, no blockchain transactions are sent, and no private keys are requested or stored. Scores describe current market conditions and are not predictions of future returns. Market conditions may change before any real execution would be possible, and simulated results will differ from actual trading results. Source code ↗ · Engineering case study
