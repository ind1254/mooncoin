Moonpaper

Privacy Policy

Effective August 24, 2026

Moonpaper is a live Solana token research application. The native iPhone app does not require an account and does not contain advertising, analytics, or cross-app tracking SDKs.

Information processed

The app sends the token searches and mint addresses you request to Moonpaper's production API so it can return market and on-chain research. Hosting and security logs may retain those searches or public mint addresses together with standard request metadata such as IP address, user agent, request path, timestamp, and diagnostic information to deliver the service, prevent abuse, and investigate failures.

Information we do not request

The iPhone app does not request wallet private keys, seed phrases, FOMO credentials, payment-card details, contacts, location, photos, microphone, camera, or advertising identifiers. It does not build, sign, or submit blockchain transactions.

Third-party links and data

Market data and token metadata may come from providers identified in the app, including Jupiter and Solana RPC infrastructure. If you choose “Trade manually on FOMO,” iOS opens a third-party FOMO URL containing the public Solana mint address. FOMO's own privacy policy and terms then apply. Moonpaper is not affiliated with FOMO Labs.

Retention and security

The native app does not create a Moonpaper account or store a personal profile. Operational logs may be retained by our infrastructure providers for limited security and reliability purposes. We use HTTPS for network requests and minimize the data sent by the app.

Your choices

You may stop all app processing by uninstalling the app. Because the native app does not create an account, there is no native-app profile to delete.

Contact

Questions or privacy requests can be submitted through the Moonpaper GitHub issue tracker.
