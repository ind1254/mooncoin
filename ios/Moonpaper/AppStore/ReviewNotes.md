# App Review notes draft

Moonpaper 1.0 is a native SwiftUI research client. It does not use WKWebView for
its primary interface. No account is required to review the app:

1. Launch the app to see the live Trending feed.
2. Switch between New and Trending.
3. Search for `BONK` and select the verified result.
4. Review the native market, chain-verification, and risk sections.
5. The "Trade manually on FOMO" control opens a third-party universal link.
   No transaction is created or submitted by Moonpaper.

The backend will remain online during review. The app contains no advertising,
tracking SDK, payment system, wallet connection, or account creation.
