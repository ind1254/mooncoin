## Description

Moonpaper helps you investigate fast-moving Solana tokens without confusing a
ticker symbol for a unique identity.

Browse live New and Trending feeds, search by name or mint address, and open a
native research report showing price, liquidity, volume, holders, authority
verification, concentration, and clearly explained risk factors.

When you decide to continue, Moonpaper can open the exact Solana mint in FOMO.
You review the token, amount, fees, slippage, and final confirmation there.
Moonpaper never connects to your wallet, requests private keys, or submits a
transaction.

Important: Moonpaper provides research and decision support, not investment
advice. Scores summarize current market conditions and do not predict future
returns. Cryptocurrency is highly volatile and may lose all of its value.

FOMO is a third-party service. Moonpaper is not affiliated with or endorsed by
FOMO Labs.
