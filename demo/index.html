FOMO Arbitrage Add-On
browser demo of the iOS feature

This phone mock-up shows exactly what ships inside the FOMO app. The purple button in the bottom-right corner is the add-on. Tap it, pick a meme coin, enter an amount, and the cloud engine compares live buy and sell prices on two different Solana venues.

If buying on one venue and selling on the other would make money after every cost — venue fees, network fees, price impact, and a safety buffer — it shows the estimated profit. Nothing is ever traded: every result is a paper calculation.
